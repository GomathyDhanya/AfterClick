# Novelty decision — 2026-11-23

Status: **INCONCLUSIVE for publication-level novelty; bounded artifact/negative-study supported.**

The original integrated story is not an established novel algorithm. Local frontier search loses
to strong coverage; all primary model-selection winners are a supplied no-learning mixture; a
learned controller ties the strongest simple timer rule at matched completion and suppression.
A fixed tree benefits from boundary data, but that does not establish superiority over the
structured baseline or broad unseen-mechanism calibration. No evolutionary or fine-tuning rescue
is justified by these results.

The strongest potential contribution is an auditable benchmark of temporal recovery contracts,
with independent committed receipts, actual adjacent PostgreSQL witnesses, a nonmonotone authority
case, a full-public-input ambiguity witness, and matched acquisition/control comparisons. This
is a candidate positioning, not proof of uniqueness. The finite learning study is deliberately
small: four fixed obligation classes, two training mechanisms and one final held-out mechanism.
The live grounding is one planted hybrid-policy case; no organic error-frequency claim follows.

CAR already establishes intervention-based replay; UndoBench overlaps strongly in dual oracles
and phase-specific fault recovery. SURE-Act already learns control from counterfactual outcomes.
Mnemosyne, Planarian, ACRFence and Reversibility Classes cover much of the systems motivation.
The source-linked matrix records what was actually read; there was no head-to-head reimplementation.

Decision: produce the reproducible paper draft as a bounded benchmark/negative evaluation, retain
the stronger simple baselines, and stop tuning this final evaluation. Do not call the broad project
research-complete, claim acceptance, or publish/push automatically. Next useful evidence is an
independent workload/schedule-distribution replication with naturally available public signals,
newly frozen evaluation instances, and the same strong baselines. This is more informative than
more samples of the present fixed laws or a larger language model.
