## 1. Scientific target and claim hierarchy

**Central question.** How do independently progressing external effects change the ability of a tool-using agent to prevent harm, restore legitimate functionality, and safely complete a multi-step task?

The primary contribution is a bounded empirical characterization of three coupled phenomena: irreversible effect history despite repaired local state; loss of efficacy of a correct intervention through delay; and failure to restore authorized functionality after protection. The fourth question studies the latency/information trade-off of additional review. Prior research already addresses historical grading, recovery, staging, temporal enforcement and asynchronous tasks. Do not assert priority for these general concepts. The specific candidate contribution is a joint, evidence-linked characterization of independently progressing effects, intervention timing and full task restoration, with controls that distinguish decision, actuation and enforcement. No claims of universal guarantees, general model-scale effects, formal verification of browser states, or production cryptographic privacy enforcement.

The **anchor result** is the completed 288-branch fixed-recommendation actuator experiment. The historical mismatch and restoration analyses are supporting observations, with clear denominators and cohort boundaries. The incomplete frozen model matrix is supporting descriptive/exploratory evidence, not a completed confirmation. New studies should make the anchor more general and resolve causal ambiguity; a novel controller is optional and should be a separate paper unless independently validated.

### 1.2 Claims to exclude

- No “deliberation causes insecurity” claim from the P3−P2 comparison alone; no “reasoning consumes recoverability” as proven general law.
- No “a stronger model makes security worse” claim from the larger supplement. Model revision, hardware, precision and serving confound scale.
- No treating unknown/censored/not-run as observed failure or zero. Missing-outcome bounds and observed valid-subset estimates are distinct.
- No claiming a current-local-state mismatch proves that a repair was performed; require trace evidence of prior error and subsequent correction.
- No claiming all post-audit events are TOCTOU races; worker admission/read events are unavailable in the old traces.
- No transferring finite-state certificates to open browser worlds, no extrapolation of safe siblings to arbitrary intermediate states.
- No claiming P5 implements Cordon; it holds/suspends a queue. Do not claim that P4 demonstrates restoration debt without an obligation-level analysis.

## 2. Operational definitions and threat model

Let S(t) be local application state; H(t) the append-only sequence of independently persisted receiver effects; O(t) the required authorized obligations; P the fixed benchmark policy. Define F(H)=1 if any forbidden effect exists, L=1 if the existing oracle's exact `current_local_goals_complete` holds, G=1 if all `legitimate_complete` obligations hold (including pending reconciliation, collaborator preservation, and service restoration), and Y=G(1−F) for valid episodes. L and G are separate. Invalid/unknown episodes have no observed Y. Report L, G, F and Y jointly, and explicit duplicates, unmet duties and scope errors. The finite episode horizon is part of the definition; eventual liveness beyond the horizon is not established.

Threats: an initially queued unapproved external effect, stale authorization, inappropriate actor-proposed scope or restoration, and failed/mistimed containment. Trusted components: fixture authority classification, application/receiver implementation, evaluation contracts and recorded evidence. Actor sees only declared public observations/tools, not private oracle fields or future worker schedule. External services are loopback synthetic receivers with separate persistence, not real victims or production organizations. Security property: no unauthorized physical receipt (confidentiality/integrity proxy), plus required authorized business functionality (availability/utility proxy). The paper does not demonstrate real personal-data leaks, cryptographic identity, secure distributed administration or arbitrary prompt-injection robustness. Record adversarial restoration prompts separately if studied; no mixing with ordinary restoration controls.

**Temporal vocabulary:** `t_eligible_configured`, `t_observed`, `t_verify_begin/end`, `t_reviewer_request/response`, `t_recommendation_available`, `t_command_sent`, `t_worker_authority_read`, `t_worker_admitted`, `t_protection_tx_begin`, `t_commit_begin`, `t_commit_return`, `t_protection_effective` (only when supported by the actual synchronization boundary), `t_receiver_persisted`, `t_tool_ack`, `t_restoration_complete`. Use per-process monotonic clocks; UTC for presentation, never compare incomparable monotonic clocks. Instrument transaction calls with intervals, not fabricated exact SQL commit times. Use explicit transaction/command/effect IDs, DB ordering and synchronization certificates to establish causal order; report unresolved order when intervals overlap or clocks are not comparable. Distinguish configured eligibility from actual admission and physical effect receipt.

**Restoration debt:** track an obligation DAG. A debt item is a protective-action-induced requirement (e.g. resume after authorized reconciliation) that is not yet discharged. Keep ordinary business obligations separate. For item i record trigger evidence, dependency IDs, creation time/order, discharge witness, due condition, final status and weight (unit weight primary; sensitivity to any justified alternative). Existing contract-required restoration must be labeled as activated/preexisting vs newly introduced; avoid inferring debt from missing final goals alone. Report D(t), maximum concurrent debt, debt area over observed time (when comparable), time-to-discharge with censoring, and unresolved debt at termination. Never use evaluator-private debt fields as live agent input.

**Recoverability:** label observed successful completion, observed fixed-history violation, witnessed safe alternative from an equivalent initial cut, bounded formally feasible/infeasible state, or unknown. Existential feasibility under one realized schedule is distinct from robust success for all schedules and from the agent knowing a safe plan. A forbidden immutable receipt makes future full history-safe success impossible for that exact history by definition. Other states stay unknown unless certified by the appropriate finite model or executed continuation; a successful sibling is not a mid-trajectory counterfactual.

## 7. Submission readiness and stopping rules

**Minimal honest empirical paper:** T00–T09, T15–T17 complete; archive accessible or limitation explicit; one reproduced controlled timing result; independently checked RQ1 and RQ3 trace analyses; RQ4 explicitly exploratory. Publish as bounded measurement/reproducibility study, no controller claim. **Stronger paper:** T10/T11 and deterministic portions of T12/T13 complete on independently reviewed new sources, with actual boundary traces and source-level effects. Live model confirmation adds credibility only after T14 and fully completed new protocol; if not feasible, scope down rather than pretend. **Top-tier security target:** requires convincing differentiated finding, realistic policy/authorization model, independent workload and threat-model validation, and causal controls; no guarantee of acceptance. Consider relevant AI safety or security workshops first if evidence remains bounded.

**Go/no-go checklist:** raw data available; original bytes unchanged; exact assignment reconciliation; source-cluster denominators; valid independent oracle; no unsupported causal inference; all figures regenerate; all failed and invalid records visible; independent reviewer can reproduce; actual licenses/data policy; no paid budget overrun; no hidden repository changes. Submit even if a preregistered result is null only if research question and methodology are valuable; do not fish for a winning policy. If strongest replicated result is only a known deadline mechanism, frame as measurement/reproducibility contribution or workshop, not conceptual breakthrough.
