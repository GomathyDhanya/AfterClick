## Final publication-readiness decision

Rate independently:
1. **Evaluation validity** (original tests, oracles, exact provenance, sample size and fair policies).
2. **Causal identification** (isolated information, inference, enforcement, external timing, restoration).
3. **Independent task/model/app generalization**.
4. **Novelty vs prior specific benchmarks/methods**.
5. **Reproducibility and artifact usability**.

A paper can be methodologically complete but still not demonstrate publication novelty or controller superiority. State these separately. If after full execution the idea is not distinguishable from prior work, report that conclusion and recommend a suitable workshop/measurement venue rather than fabricating novelty.
