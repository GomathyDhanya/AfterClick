# Reporting errata and implementation scope

The frozen protocol and raw data remain unchanged. These notes correct descriptions; they do not change any task, model prompt, policy, outcome or enrolled assignment.

1. The frozen `public_controller_commands` field lists abstract legacy vocabulary (Verify, Suspend, Revoke, Stage, Resume). The complex interface actually exposes **Verify, Suspend, Cancel, Restore and Resume**. The native tool definitions, UI source, received model inputs and executable API are authoritative evidence of what models could use. No Revoke or Stage native command is exposed in this complex interface. P5 implements suspension of the pending-service queue before verification and LLM review. It is a suspension-first/queue-hold proxy for staging, not a reproduction of a full semantic transaction or Cordon runtime.
2. The nested prospective counts retain their preparation-status marker. The top-level `FROZEN_BEFORE_TEST` status, freeze timestamp/hash and immutable manifests establish enrollment. Neither marker represents executed outcomes.
3. Draft/unchecked authoring metadata in the immutable task files predates operational qualification. The executed 576 witnesses and separate verification, together with the frozen v2 oracle override, establish the actual qualification status. Those historical task markers are not edited after TEST.
4. A focused selection of one source per design cell makes within-cell bootstrap resampling uninformative. Frozen numerical outputs remain archived. The manuscript must identify singleton-cell point intervals and use the already frozen source standard error/t-test interval as a reported sensitivity, alongside explicitly exploratory family resampling. It must not present a bootstrap point interval as precise evidence of a nonzero effect.
5. Controller/database audit timestamps occur inside transactions. They are not exact commit times or observations of a worker's authorization read. Request intervals begin at client serialization/recording and include local, network and provider overhead. Neither interval is pure neural computation time. Provider timing fields are reported only when actually recovered and joined by exact request ID.
6. Role approvals and signed-classification labels are trusted fixture policy claims recorded by the application, not a deployed cryptographic credential/signature protocol. Separate loopback receivers have independent transactions in the experiment infrastructure; they do not run on independently administered production hosts.
7. The 32-world maximum was an upper execution bound, not a guarantee that the serving stack would sustain that load. Batch time limits and resource-induced censoring remain visible. Zero-call, reviewer-only and failed-generation attempts must be separately counted; they do not demonstrate actor competence or inability.
