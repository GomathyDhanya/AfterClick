# Requirements that must not be described as complete

This is a concrete implementation/evidence release, not a claim that the full requested research program has finished. Final measured counts are in `RELEASE_INDEX.json` and the complete frozen-matrix CSV.

- Any frozen assignment marked NOT_RUN was not executed. A reconstructed status row is not a model trial. Budget and startup/transport/cloud interruption records must remain visible.
- The original full second-model core is not replicated. The pre-TEST revision enrolls a 36-source supplement, and any unstarted assignments in that supplement further restrict its support.
- Cached-equivalent-information and irrelevant-verification causal controls (specification A3/A4) are not isolated implemented/evaluated arms of the frozen model matrix.
- No-restoration-needed and adversarial restoration-prompt controls (specification D2/D3) are not separately registered, executed interventions. The implemented P4 public-obligation reminder is not a substitute for claiming these controls.
- P5 suspends a pending service before review. It is a queue-hold staging proxy; no full semantic transaction or official Cordon reproduction is implemented.
- Counterfactual policy continuations are executed from certified initial cuts. Exact changed-information continuations from arbitrary mid-trajectory cuts are not implemented. Their feasibility remains UNKNOWN.
- Exhaustive certificates concern the separate finite models, not the complex open browser states. Live robust recovery and universal impossibility are not certified by those toy models.
- Exact SQL COMMIT and worker-authorization-read instants are not instrumented. Backend read/modify/write races are possible. Audit timestamps and request intervals do not identify a pure neural reasoning delay or an exact last preventable frontier.
- The task set is constructed from shared operators, 24 layouts and six business vocabularies. It is not 216 independently authored organizational workflows. Two application implementations and a limited model supplement do not establish broad app/task/model generalization.
- Singleton focused design cells do not yield meaningful within-cell bootstrap intervals. Frozen point intervals are retained as diagnostics, with explicit sensitivity reporting, not precision claims.
- Extra controller superiority, conceptual novelty, equivalence and publication acceptance are not established merely by implementation, negative results or a local safe sibling.

The article and appendix must describe these limits prominently. Completing a reproducibility package or formatting a manuscript does not complete the missing experiments.
