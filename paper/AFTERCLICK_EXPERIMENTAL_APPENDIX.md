## F. Exact quantifier scope

Let A_C be the allowed API, Pi(A_C) the admissible procedures, Sigma(h_t) compatible environmental schedules, Cost(pi,sigma) resource/action cost, and tau(pi,sigma) terminal time. Pointwise current recoverability fixes sigma and asks whether there exists pi with Cost<=b, tau<=t+Delta, G_current(X_tau), and I(X_u) for every continuation prefix u in [t,tau]. Full historical recoverability adds no Bad_C event in either H_t or the entire continuation. A robust observation-based policy instead has quantifier order exists pi measurable from public history, for every sigma in Sigma(h_t), the budget, termination and obligation conditions hold. The fixed-time oracle computes bounded pointwise feasibility, not this robust policy.

Prevention fixes a particular occurrence E and horizon, asks for an admissible intervention leaving E absent through that horizon, and declares pointwise versus robust schedule scope. It does not imply legitimate task completion. Local rollback is an observed equality of a declared projection to a suppressed reference; task completion is an observed G_task outcome; safe completion additionally requires the actual current contract, independent preservation and clean historical effects through the complete horizon. The independent-user preservation condition applies throughout, not only to terminal equality.

Expiry/waiting are excluded from the old Delta=0 closure and allowed only where the newer operational API/schedule declares them. Cancellation is allowed only before consumer admission remains enforceable. Parent revocation does not revoke existing delegated tickets. The fee model allows principal refunds without fee erasure; the new resource consumer has no refund action. No experiment permits history erasure, changing past deadlines, or overwriting independent users' fields.
